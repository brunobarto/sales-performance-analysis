# sales-performance-analysis

How to use: 

after the loading bar, go through the 7 numbered charts in order. Each one answers a business question, and clicking a card opens a pop-up with three parts: what it shows, key insight and business action. The four recommendation cards at the bottom sum up the story.

Interactions: hover any bar, point or slice for exact values. Your language, theme and accent color are saved in the browser, so they stay set when you reload the page.

Main insights
Revenue grew 13% year over year, with a strong Q4 peak.
France brings 28% of revenue.
Customer value follows a Pareto pattern: 18% of customers bring 60% of revenue.
Vinyl brings in the traffic, while hi-fi equipment makes the large baskets.
